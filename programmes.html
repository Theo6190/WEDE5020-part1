Our Programmes

From community kitchens to nutrition education — discover how we create lasting change.

PROGRAMME 1

Community Food Bank

We collect and distribute non-perishable food items to over 200 families in need across Cape Town townships every month. We partner with local stores to rescue food that would otherwise go to waste.

Donate Food

PROGRAMME 2

Community Kitchens

We operate 8 community kitchens across Khayelitsha, serving 1,500+ hot meals monthly to children, the elderly, and vulnerable families. Each meal includes a protein, vegetable, and starch.

Volunteer

PROGRAMME 3

Nutrition & Skills Education

We teach families how to grow their own vegetables and cook nutritious meals on a budget. This programme empowers communities to become food secure and self-sufficient long-term.
