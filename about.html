ROOTED IN COMMUNITY

Our Story

Hearts of Hope Charity was founded in 2023 by community members in Khayelitsha. What started in a church hall with 10 volunteers and a mission to feed 50 families has grown into a network of 8 community kitchens.

Today we serve over 200 families monthly, providing daily meals, food parcels, and nutrition education to create lasting change across Cape Town.

OUR MISSION

To End Hunger in Cape Town

We believe that everyone deserves access to nutritious food and the dignity that comes with food security.
